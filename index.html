OUR STORY

Welcome to Amalfi

We believe great pizza brings people together. Inspired by the rich flavors of Italy and the relaxed island lifestyle of Bali, Amalfi celebrates the art of Italian pizza, made with passion and quality ingredients.

Alongside our Italian dishes, refreshing cocktails, and carefully selected drinks, we creat a welcoming atmosphere where great food, good music, and great company come together.

Whether you're joining us for a casual lunch, a romantic dinner, or an evening with friends, Amalfi is the perfect place to enjoy a little taste of Italy.
